Background

I studied computer science, mathematics and statistics, then spent my time since learning the half of the job that coursework skips: getting an application off a laptop and onto a server where strangers can reach it safely. I pick problems that have little to do with each other on purpose - a containerised expense tracker, a handwriting-recognition pipeline, a statistical study of a century of Olympic results - because the unfamiliar part is where the learning is. In practice that means I am as comfortable debugging a reverse proxy or an IAM policy as I am writing the application code sitting behind it.
